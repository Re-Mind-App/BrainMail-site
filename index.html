BrainMail

BrainMail lets users save personal thoughts, reflections, and reminders, then receive their own saved entries back by text message over time.

About BrainMail

BrainMail is a self-reflection app for saving thoughts worth remembering. Users can write short entries, affirmations, ideas, or moments of clarity and receive those saved entries back later as reminder text messages.

BrainMail only sends text messages to users who voluntarily provide their phone number and affirmatively consent during onboarding inside the app.

Use case category: Customer Care / Productivity — self-authored reminder messages.

How BrainMail works

1. Save entries

Users write and save their own personal entries inside the app.

2. Receive texts

BrainMail can text saved entries back to the user based on their settings and activity.

3. Stay in control

Users can manage reminders in the app and can opt out of SMS messages at any time.

SMS consent and opt-in

BrainMail requires explicit in-app consent before sending SMS reminder messages. During onboarding, users enter their phone number and must actively check an unchecked consent box before continuing.

Current in-app consent language:

I agree to receive reminder text messages from BrainMail.
Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for support.

The phone signup screen also states: “Your number is only used for verification and your BrainMail texts.”

STOP

Replying STOP unsubscribes the user from BrainMail SMS reminder messages.

HELP

Replying HELP provides support information and messaging assistance.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the user’s settings, saved entries, subscription status, and activity.

Message and data rates may apply. SMS delivery may depend on the user’s mobile carrier, device, and network availability.

Privacy and phone number use

BrainMail uses phone numbers only for account verification and BrainMail text messages.

  • BrainMail does not sell phone numbers or personal information.
  • BrainMail does not send third-party advertising or marketing SMS campaigns.
  • Saved personal entries are private unless the user voluntarily posts content to the public Community section.
  • Users may delete their account in the app.

For more information, please review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service linked below.

Community Guidelines

BrainMail includes an optional public Community section where users may share reflections with other users. To keep this space safe and welcoming, the following content is not allowed:

  • Hate speech, slurs, or discrimination
  • Harassment, bullying, or threats
  • Sexually explicit or pornographic content
  • Violence or encouragement of harm
  • Spam, scams, or misleading content
  • Impersonation or posting on behalf of others
  • Content that violates applicable law or the rights of others

Users may report posts that violate these guidelines. Reported content may be reviewed and removed, and repeated or serious violations may result in account restriction or removal.
